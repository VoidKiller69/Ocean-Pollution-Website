Save the Oceans

Welcome to this website which is dedicated to helping protect our oceans and educate people about ocean pollution!

This website is designed to raise awareness about the importance of ocean conservation and provide information on how ocean pollution affects the oceans so that people can make a difference to our oceans. In this website you will learn about kaitiakitanga, How the oceans are being affected by ocean pollution, and some things that you can do to help protect and save our oceans.
